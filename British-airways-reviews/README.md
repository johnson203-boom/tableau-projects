#### British Airways Customer Reviews Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard exploring customer feedback for British Airways between March 2016 and October 2023. I built this project to turn raw passenger review data into a clear, self-service tool that makes it easy to spot trends in service quality, fleet performance, and customer satisfaction. 

 

#### Overview

Instead of sifting through thousands of individual reviews, this dashboard brings everything together into one interactive view: 

* **At-a-Glance KPIs**: Tracks average scores across Overall Rating, Cabin Staff Service, Entertainment, Food & Beverages, Ground Service, Seat Comfort, and Value for Money.
* **Dynamic Metric Selector**: A built-in parameter control on the left lets users switch which metric drives all the charts simultaneously.
* **Performance Over Time**: Visualizes monthly rating trends from 2016 to 2023 to highlight shifts in passenger sentiment.
* **Country & Aircraft Breakdowns**: Maps average ratings by reviewer country and compares satisfaction scores alongside review volume across different aircraft models.
* **Interactive Filters**: Allows slicing the data by Seat Type (Economy, Business, First, etc.), Traveller Type, Aircraft, and Continent.

#### Key Insights

* **Wide-Body Advantage**: Long-haul aircraft like the Boeing 787, 747, and 777 earn noticeably higher average ratings than narrow-body planes like the A321.
* **People Over Perks**: Cabin staff and ground service receive much stronger ratings than inflight entertainment and food, particularly among Economy Class flyers.

#### Tools & Techniques Used

* **Tableau Desktop**: Parameter-driven measures, calculated fields, and custom data grouping (standardizing aircraft model names)
* **Dashboard Design**: Clean layout hierarchy, interactive filtering, and an intentional color palette tied to British Airways' branding
